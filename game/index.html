How to play

WASD / Arrows Move on foot · Drive (W accelerate, S brake/reverse, A/D steer)
Mouse Aim your pistol while on foot
E Get in / out of the nearest car (stealing a car raises your wanted level)
Space / Click Punch (fists) or shoot (pistol)
Q Switch between fists and pistol
Shift Sprint on foot
M Mission list — start the next job
P / Esc Pause · H This help

Wanted level: crimes (stealing cars, hitting people, shooting) raise your stars. Police cars chase and ram you; officers chase you on foot and will bust you if they catch you standing still. Break line of sight and hide to cool the heat down — when the stars hit zero, you are clear.

Missions: press M and pick the next job, or just explore. The yellow diamond on the map and minimap is your objective. Completing a mission pays cash. Finish all five to complete the story.

Pickups: green cash, red health kits, pistol crates, and gold packages are scattered around the city — walk or drive over them.
